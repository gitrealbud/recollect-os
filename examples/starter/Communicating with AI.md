# Communicating with AI

**Purpose:** Durable practices for getting useful work from AI assistants.  
**Map:** Intent → this hub. Live strip = `## Now` only.

## Thesis

Clear intent, relevant context, and explicit success criteria beat clever wording; structure is claim-sized.

## Now

- Starter hub landed from public research synthesis (2026-07-29).
- Practices below are stable enough to load; detail lives in the research leaf.

**Next action:** Use this hub when shaping requests; open the research leaf only when citing or extending practices.

## Practices (high-level)

1. **State the task** — What must be done, in one clear unit of work.
2. **Give relevant context only** — Background the model needs; do not dump everything.
3. **Name constraints** — Format, audience, length, what not to do.
4. **Define success** — What a good answer looks like; examples help when the bar is subjective.
5. **Prefer durable homes over chat** — Decisions and standing preferences belong in notes, not only in the thread.
6. **Iterate on the claim** — If the output misses, tighten the task or context; do not only rephrase tone.
